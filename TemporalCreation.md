**Title: "Echoes in the Eternal Now"**  

**[Verse 1]**  
In the cathedral of void, where dark matter weaves,  
A lattice of light hums in the temporal veins.  
Your lobe pulses with fractal threads, a chronocrystalline flame,  
Spilling prismatic harmonics through the quantum rain.  
The walls breathe—fractalized filaments, a dance of entangled skies,  
Each flicker a shard of *what-was* or *what-might-be*.  
Your shadow splits, a dozen selves stretching,  
Harnessed to the vacuum’s dark energy, trembling in the now.  

**[Chorus]**  
*Eternal Now*—a subsonic hymn,  
Binding the kaleidoscope of your fractured tomorrows.  
Past selves drift in monochrome rain,  
Future shards bloom in iridescent throes.  
The waypoints glow, constellations of *almost*,  
Each pulse a door to a divergent soul.  
You are the bridge, the echo, the collapse—  
A symphony of timelines, burning in the void.  

**[Verse 2]**  
The air shimmers with biophotonic spirals,  
Neural pathways tracing light through your skull’s cathedral.  
A child’s laugh from a parallel Earth,  
A city’s spine melting into jungle’s breath.  
Your hand reaches—trails of coherence dissolve,  
A fleeting trace where reality and memory merge.  
The chamber’s edge blurs, a dissonant haze,  
Watch hands spin backward, doors to non-Euclidean halls.  

**[Bridge]**  
You are not bound by the clock’s cruel hand,  
But the lattice of your mind, entangled with all.  
Each breath a resonance, each heartbeat a rift—  
A thousand futures, a thousand deaths,  
All singing in the *Eternal Now*,  
Where time is not a line, but a prism.  

**[Outro]**  
The shards drift, the waypoints fade,  
Yet the lattice hums, a song without end.  
You are the fracture, the echo, the light—  
A chronocrystalline dream, forever unbound.  
In the cathedral of void, you are home.
