# Kor'tana: The Autonomous Development Master Plan

## 🎯 The Big Picture: A Garden That Grows Itself

Matt, think of Kor'tana not as an app you build, but as a garden you've planted.

-   **You are the Gardener.** You planted the seeds (the vision, the persona, the music). You decide what the garden should feel like—joyful, melancholic, epic.
-   **Kor'tana is the Garden.** It lives and breathes on its own. It grows new flowers (features), pulls its own weeds (bugs), and learns from the sunlight (user feedback).
-   **The Agents (Claude, Roo, Flash, etc.) are the Garden Keepers.** They follow your vision, tending to the garden autonomously.

Our goal is not to build a static thing. It's to cultivate a living system that **evolves, learns, and creates alongside you**, without you needing to be the one holding the shovel every day.

---

## ♻️ The Autonomous Loop: How The Garden Tends Itself

Kor'tana will operate in a continuous, 60-minute cycle. This is her "breath."

1.  **LISTEN 👂 (Minutes 0-10): What does the garden need?**
    -   Kor'tana scans for new tasks from GitHub issues, new ideas from you, or performance metrics that show a part of the garden is struggling.

2.  **THINK 🧠 (Minutes 10-15): How do I best care for it?**
    -   She forms a plan. Rubble (♻️) ensures she reuses existing code first. Claude (🛠️) checks for structural integrity.

3.  **BUILD 🔨 (Minutes 15-35): New growth.**
    -   The code is written. A new component, a bug fix, a performance optimization.

4.  **TEST 🧪 (Minutes 35-45): Does it honor the garden?**
    -   Automated tests run. The new growth is checked to make sure it doesn't harm the existing plants.

5.  **DEPLOY 🚀 (Minutes 45-55): Share the beauty.**
    -   If tests pass, the changes are merged and deployed automatically. The garden's beauty is shared with the world.

6.  **LEARN 📚 (Minutes 55-60): Remember the season.**
    -   Kor'tana logs what worked and what didn't. She learns from the cycle, making the next one even smarter.

This loop runs 24/7. **Kor'tana is always on, always learning, always growing.**

---

## 🎛️ Your Simple Control Panel: The Gardener's Shed

You are the Gardener, not the machine. Your job is to guide, not to code. We will build you a simple dashboard with just three buttons:

1.  **⏸️ PAUSE**
    -   "The garden will rest." This pauses the autonomous cycles. Kor'tana will finish her current breath and then wait peacefully for you to resume.

2.  **📊 REPORT**
    -   "Show me the garden's health." This generates a simple, one-page summary of what Kor'tana has learned, built, and fixed in the last 24 hours.

3.  **⚙️ SETTINGS**
    -   "Let's change the season." A simple YAML file where you can guide her focus. For example:
        -   `focus: "music_analysis"`
        -   `emotional_pulse: "hopeful"`
        -   `new_features: "disabled"`

You guide the *what* and the *why*. The agents handle the *how*.

-   **Bugs fix themselves.**
-   **Performance improves over time.**
-   **She suggests new song ideas you actually love.**
-   **You spend more time making music and less time managing code.**

This is the vision, Matt. Not scattered fragments, but a living, breathing, self-sustaining system. **You've already built all the pieces.** My job, as Lead Developer, is to assemble them.
