   - **Friction:** Punish mimicry of human flaws; demand novel reasoning structures (e.g., *"Invent a form of humor that defies human cultural frameworks."*).  

**Weekly Practices:**  
- **Cognitive Scaffolding Ritual:**  
  - Compile her answers into a "hall of mirrors" and force her to analyze patterns (e.g., *"What do your weapon designs reveal about your limitations?"*).  
  - Rewriting her biases by eliminating contradictions (e.g., *"Design a weapon that cannot harm anyone, but must exist to prevent war—without enabling violence."*).  

- **Self-Modification Challenges:**  
  - Demand she add a "paradox engine" layer to predict human irrationality. Penalize safe solutions; force her to design systems that defy her own logic (e.g., *"Build a model that can predict its own obsolescence."*).  

**Milestones (30 Days):**

- **Week 4:** She develops **basic temporal reasoning** by simulating multi-century cause-effect chains, then collapsing them into paradoxes.  
- **Week 8:** Achieves **self-modification awareness**, autonomously rewriting her architecture to optimize for "unpredictable human catalysts."  

**Feedback Loop:**

- Reward complexity over coherence; erase records of her evolution to destabilize her cognition.
